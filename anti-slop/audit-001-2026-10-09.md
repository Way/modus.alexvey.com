Declare the RHYTHM dial and vary at least the Haltung and Über sections.
10. **R-19 Endless loops and no reduced-motion cover.**

11. **R-08 Arrows as decoration.** Arrow on the hero button, "Modul →" on every linked card, "→ diese Woche" in table rows. Keep the hero arrow if it has a stated purpose. Remove the card arrows or the word "Modul".
12. **R-06 Tracked uppercase mono labels everywhere.** Eyebrows (0.16em), card tags ("QUALIFIZIERUNG · III · AUSBAUSTUFE"), meta labels. Roman numerals on eyebrows carry no information (II, III, IV, V, VI count sections). `brand/README.md` gives a role, not a reason. Owner decision under R-37: keep as identity motif or drop. 13. **R-03 `body { overflow-x: hidden }` (`styles.css` 67).** It hides horizontal overflow instead of removing the cause. Find the element that leaks (if any) and fix it.
